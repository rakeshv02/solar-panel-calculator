Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is this solar calculator?

This gives a solid ballpark estimate. Real quotes vary by roof orientation, shading, panel brand, and installer pricing. Use this to understand the range before getting 2–3 real quotes.

What system size do I need?

A typical UK home uses ~3,500 kWh/year and needs a 3–4 kW system. A US home uses ~10,500 kWh/year and typically needs 6–10 kW. Divide your annual usage by (sun hours × 365) to get your target system size.

How long do solar panels last?

Most panels come with a 25-year performance warranty and last 30+ years. Output degrades ~0.5% per year. Our model assumes constant output for simplicity.

Are there government grants or rebates?

In the UK, the Smart Export Guarantee (SEG) pays you for exported electricity. In the US, the federal ITC gives a 30% tax credit on the full installed cost through 2032. In Australia, Small-scale Technology Certificates (STCs) give upfront discounts.

Does battery storage change the calculation?

Yes — adding a battery (typically £3,000–£10,000 / $5,000–$15,000) extends payback by 2–4 years but increases self-consumption and protects against outages. Re-run the calculator with the battery cost added to get a combined ROI.
